by Blair Stannard

Transportation and Tourism:
 
Air access  to SNA could not be simpler.  Our newly opened John Wayne Airport (airport designator code = SNA) can be seen at:  http://www.ocair.com/

Maps: 

of SNA are available through our subsidiary the Swedish National Atlas 
(SNA) at:  http://www.sna.se/webatlas/index.html

A SNA Yearbook:  

full of useful information on our favourite country, is published each Fall. It is fully accredited with Le Syndicat National des Annuaires : http://www.sna.asso.fr/
 
Crafts for Tourists:  

A wide selection of traditional SNA crafts are available at: http://snacrafts.tripod.com/snacrafts/  

Business:

The people of SNA are entrepreneurs.  They even have favourite stock listed on the NY Stock Exchange: 
http://www.google.com/search?hl=en&prev=/search%3Fq%3Dsna%26hl%3Den%26sa%3DN
&q=stocks:SNA+

Health Care:

Health Care for citizens is free (state paid):  Services are provided by SNA Healthcare: http://www.sna.org.sg/ 

Literacy:

Canada has entered into a programme to increase literacy amongst SNA
citizens:  http://www.nald.ca/nlsf.htm

Recreation:

SNA citizens love flowers and gardens:  http://www.sna.org/ They also enjoy an evening at the cinema:  http://www.sna.net.au/

Hobbies: 

SNA citizens, of Dutch background, have a great interest in archaeology:
http://www.sna.nl/
Some of those of French and Swiss background equally enjoy astronomy:

Industries - Informatics: 

SNA is a leading country in Informatics technology.  Even Microsoft has tried to follow in the footsteps of a leader: http://www.microsoft.com/hiserver/evaluation/previousversions/ 
 
In fact, SNA is so advanced in this field, that Yale University has written a paper on "Introduction to SNA":  http://www.yale.edu/pclt/COMM/SNA.HTM

Local Consulting services are provided through:  http://www.snanet.co.uk/

In 2003, SNA will be hosting an International Conference (SNA 2003 ) on Informatics, in Paris, France:  Info on SNA 2003 is available at: http://sna-2003.cea.fr/

Industries - Airplane Manufacture:

See our own Seawind SNA made models at:  http://www.seawindsna.com/ 
 
Industries - CD and  DVD manufacture:

For 50 years, we have been a leader.  Our products have included : Audiocassette, then
Audio Compact disc and CD Rom and finally DVD are now part of our range of recorded media. see: http://www.snacompactdisc.com/frameang.html
 
Industries - Beekeeping (Honey):  
 
Always a country with a sweet tooth, SNA beekeepers have built a great honey industry.
They mostly share their findings with their colleagues in France.
