NEW YEAR 2006

Answering again the popular demand, the SNA Postal Administration has issued a self-adhesive sheet dedicated to the end of this prosperous year and to the arrival of the next one. The theme chosen for this year's issue is the SNA national animal, the fennec, shown in its natural winter environment.

The issuing day of the sheet was the 16th of December 2005. The sheets were designed by the Stamp Concept company, located in the capital, Ciudad de Leon. They were printed, as usually, by the security office Courvoisier SNA. The print run is of 20,050 pieces only.

In the name of the SNA and of its postal administration we wish you Happy Holidays!
