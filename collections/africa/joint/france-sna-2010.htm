JOINT ISSUE FRANCE - SNA

The 64th Salon philatélique d'automne (Fall Philatelic Salon) opens at 10 am on November 4 in Paris, at the Porte de Champerret. Among many guests, the Salon will be visited by the participants in the philatelic newsgroup news:fr.rec.philatelie.

The guests of honor at the Salon are the postal administrations of Russia and of the SNA. For this event SNAPS (SNA Postal Service) prepared and will sell the Joint Issue France - SNA miniature sheet displayed below. It will be available only at the Salon, for the price of Euro 1.20.

The print run of this issue is of 10'000 sheets only. They were printed by the security printer Courvoisier SNA at Ciudad de Leon, the capital of the SNA.You cannot miss the SNA booth, that looks very similar to this one:
