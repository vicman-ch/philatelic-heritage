INTENTIONS & RESULTS

(or how the PAs contribute to stamp collecting)

I have recently created several web pages, in which I have used some stamp related material that I have received toward the end of the year 2005. One of them presented covers and cards specially created or cancelled for the stamp collectors.

Another page presented the 2005 redenomination of the Romania currency, as reflected on covers received from non-philatelists in the months of November and December 2005.

I leave toi the readers to judge the quality of the cancellations for both cathegories of sendings.

Here I'd like to show you just two fragments of covers that were send on the same day, the 28 December 2005, one from the small city of Aarberg in Switzerland and the other one from New York, USA. Both were sent by people who know that I'm a stamp collector, so they tried to make me happy. Here I would like to thank them a lot, and to show the results of their efforts.

I'l start by the Swiss cover, that displays a comics by the Swiss artist Uli Stein. The stamp appeared on the 8th of March 2005, and it shows "The impertinent mouse writing a letter". I suppose that any stamp collector will appreciate not only the Emmethaler letter that comes out from the typewriter but also the franked cover that is already prepared for the sending. The cancel is clear and clean.

And now let's take a look at a letter from a dear friend of mine from New York City.It contents (a nice greeting card) perfectly satisfied the Country Conditions for Mailing to Switzerland, and for this reason it didn't contain: arms and weapons, human remains, live plants and animals, lottery tickets and advertisements for lotteries, mini-spies or radar detectors (as specified by http://pe.usps.gov/text/imm/immicl/immiclss_025.html )

The letter is overpaid, with a total of $1.48 instead of the required $0.80 for an Airmail Letter Post. This was obviously made in order to deliver me a joy for the holidays, what perfectly (even if not purposely) fits into the policy of the USPS marketing, as follows:

DELIVER THE JOY!
NEW HOLIDAY ORNAMENTS STAMPS ISSUED BY U.S. POSTAL SERVICE AND AND
LORD & TAYLOR UNVEILED ITS CHRISTMAS WINDOWS SALUTING THE U.S. POSTAL SERVICE

"Holiday cards, letters and gifts carry seasonal messages, but the season's greetings really begin on the outside, on the envelope or package," said John Nolan, Deputy Postmaster General, U.S. Postal Service. "Over 140 million Holiday Ornaments stamps will grace a bevy of envelopes and packages as they travel across the country this holiday season, and it is our hope that the stamps will deliver love, peace and joy."
http://www.usps.com/communications/news/stamps/2004.htm

Such a pity that my joy was practically annihilated by the cancellation. Not only that it is covers with its traditional dirty waves about 3/4 of the surface of two stamps, but other two stamps aren't cancelled at all. If the reason of such heavy cancels is to hinder them from being reused (taking the risk that the resulted stamps won't "deliver love, peace and joy") then for 50% of the stamps shown above the attempt totally failed.
