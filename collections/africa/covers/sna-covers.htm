The SNA Philatelic Covers

Important is to cancel at time 

A precious forerunner of the SNA philately is shown below. These are the only pieces found so far on a really circulated cover, and regularly cancelled by the USPS. Please notice the classic style of the SNA issue, that makes grow pale the colored, but million-times issued stamps found in their proximity.

A superb letter sent from the Consulate-General of Spanish North Africa in Canada by the General Consul to Switzerland. Both sheets were cancelled by a common Canadian cancel on 2001.VII.1, in Ottawa (please take a look at details below, on the right). It's a rare SNA letter from webmaster's personal collection.

 

See below a very interesting historical cover, sent in 1939 to the actress Shirley Temple from German North Africa, at that time under the strong philatelic influence of Spanish North Africa.

The SNA - Switzerland joint sheet was very popular indeed. For this reason it can be found cancelled also on a cover sent by Air Mail, on 8/8/2001, from Ottawa (Canada) to France. 

We hope that this page will give a definitive answer to all questions concerning the real circulation and the universal validity of SNA postal issues.
