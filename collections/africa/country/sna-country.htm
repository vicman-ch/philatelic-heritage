In Memoriam — My Friend Blair David Stannard

September 27, 1946 – May 23, 2026

In loving memory of my friend Blair David Stannard, of Ottawa, Canada, whose friendship I shall always cherish.

Born in Montreal, Blair devoted much of his professional life to Canada's federal public service and served as Vice-President of the Professional Institute of the Public Service of Canada (PIPSC).

A man of wide-ranging interests and intellectual curiosity, Blair was a devoted supporter of the arts, particularly ballet, and a long-time patron of Ottawa's National Arts Centre. He was a member of Mensa and of the Monarchist League of Canada.

A lifelong philatelist, he had a passion for stamp collecting that began in childhood and remained with him throughout his life. In recognition of his dedication to the field, he was also an honorary member of the Royal Philatelic Society of Canada.

His interests also embraced local history and a special affection for cats.

Blair also helped preserve the images featured on this page for posterity. His contribution remains a cherished part of the history of this website.

I dedicate these lines to his memory, with gratitude for his friendship and with deep sadness at his passing.

Victor Manta
