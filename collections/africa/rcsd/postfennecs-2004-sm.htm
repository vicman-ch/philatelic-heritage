Spanish North Africa Postfennecs

   Answering the popular demand, the SNA Postal Organization (SNAPO) has re-issued the set of two souvenir sheets dedicated to the coming year 2004.
 


 The sheets were designed by the Stamp Concept company, located in Ciudad de Leon. They were printed, as usually, by the security office Courvoisier SNA. The print run is of 5,000 pieces only.
