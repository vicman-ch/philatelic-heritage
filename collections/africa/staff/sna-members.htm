An Invitation To All SNA Collectors

Important is to participate (P.d.C.) 

The 5th SNA Philatelic Congress, organized by the SNA Philatelic Society in collaboration with the Fennec University of  the SNA, will take place between 12/31and 1/1 in the capital of SNA, Ciudad de Leon.

The participants will be accommodated in the flats shown below, on both images.

The number of places being strictly limited, all SNA collectors who are interested in a participation should contact ASAP the Organization Committee of the Congress.

The following activities were announced so far:

  • STANNARD, Blair. Paper presentation: "The SNA world domination."
     

  • MANTA, Victor. Workshop: "Designing and printing stamps in the SNA desert"; with practical exercises.
     

  • BARBER, Tracy. Round Table: "Give a SNA stamp to a kid!"
     

  • AMBRAJA, Ralph. Lecture: "Varieties of the SNA - France (Fennecs - Crazy Horses) Joint Issue." The live presentation is in preparation.
     

  • MAISTRE, Didier. Paper: "SNA comme moteur · explosion de la philat·lie moderne." Translation assured.
     

  • HAWK, Neil S. Speech: "Viva la SNA!". Translation assured.
     

  • COURTIADE, Pierre. Seminar: "The Political Correctness of SNA stamp issues".
     

  • KYHL, Kristian. Thesis: "The Faked Issues of German North Africa".

A special cancel that will commemorate the 5th SNA PC Congress is in preparation at the SNAPO.

See you soon in Ciedade de Leon! Brillez pas par votre absence!
