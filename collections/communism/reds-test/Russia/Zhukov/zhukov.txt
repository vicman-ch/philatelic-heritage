Most of us, Russian speakers are used to the WWII story line that goes as following: Hitler came, started to advance, thanks to the will of the Soviet people and the ingenuity of its leadership we have overcome the enemy, praise General Zhukov and all those Soviet people who gave their lives at war!!...Which is true in some sense. However, what the common man have never ever would even dare to think about - is that the above summary was carefully concucted, and promoted during the Krushevian era of Zastoi, when Stalin necessarily had to look evil. Otherwise who would take the blame for the millions of deaths?

Victor Suvorov looks deeper into the history, and the sources that it arrives from. There is no doubt that "history is written by the victors". Suvorov challanges that with his brave criticism of Zhukov, such as providing insights into his personality, and his ability as a marshall. Victor Suvorov overviews some of the most controversial events and battles of the War, with the purpose of singling out the cause for failure, if there was such, or at least simply asking, "Why have so many people have perished? Was that really necessary?"

Victor Suvorov as i understand has many critics, especially in Russia that he has abandoned (at least physically). Im not one of them. I think it is more than necessary to know the truth of what has happened back then. And if this is not the ultimate of it, at least it can bring us closer to that understanding. Im honored to be writing the 1st Amazon review on this book.
