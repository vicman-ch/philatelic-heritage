BULL HEADS OF MOLDOVA 

Bull Heads 75th Anniversary, 1858 - 1932

    On 20th of November 1932 the Romanian postal administration has commemorated the 75th anniversary of first stamps of Romanian Principality Moldova by a set of 7 imperforated stamps. The first Moldavian stamps are shown on the first and the second stamps displayed below. The others display coat of arms of the state and of provinces.

    The print run was of 200,000 sets. Michel 450 - 456, Scott 421 - 427.

Bull Heads Centenary, 1858 - 1958

    The Romanian post has issued in 1958 two sets and two sheets in commemoration of the centenary of Bull's Heads. They were issued in same colors, with one perforated and the other one imperforated (the imperfs are shown above).

     The subjects of the stamps are: Postillon blowing horn, courier on horseback, Scissors cutting strips of 1858 stamps, Postillon mail coach,  Printer and hand press, various denominations of 1858 issue with Romanian rugs as background.

    The Souvenir Sheets shows the only known tête-bêche pair of 27 parale, conserved at the Library of the Romanian Academy (probably a fake).

    Just to mention that, being so expensive, I couldn't buy the sheets when the were issued, this because at that time I was a school boy and just a starting stamp collector. So I bought only the sets, that were some of the first mint stamps of my collection. In the meantime I have changed my collecting area. The above sheets entered my collection 40 years later (thanks to an Internet friend), together with the overprinted blue sheet (1959) dedicated to "10 years of state philatelic commerce" (not shown, because  not so interesting, even if scarce).
