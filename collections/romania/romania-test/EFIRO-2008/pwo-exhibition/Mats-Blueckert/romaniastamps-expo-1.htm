When I was introduced to this area
I was fascinated by the fact that the only available information was an out- of- print book issued 1945 in Portugal and in French.

The great philatelist Edouard Cohen said:
"I have here only offered a starting point for a future classification and description".

There is still much to be added, discovered, classified and described. Add to the book!
