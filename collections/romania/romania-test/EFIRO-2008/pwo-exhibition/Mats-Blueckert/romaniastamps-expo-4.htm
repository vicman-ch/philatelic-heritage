THE FUTURE

Romaniastamps is one of the best nation-specific web
pages in the world. We have over 600 unique visitors each day.
Even so you might at times get angry at a page with bad design or lack of information or..........You know you could do better! So, please do!

A book is limited by it´s covers, the web is infinite and is built on links. As for now we use 25% of our storage capacity. Scan your exhibition display - write articles - be a part of something world class - I could do with a couple of sub-editors :-)

Information sparks interest. Free, detailed information on Romanian philately helps new collectors, raises value of collections and, finally, the best way to learn something is to make a webpage about it.
