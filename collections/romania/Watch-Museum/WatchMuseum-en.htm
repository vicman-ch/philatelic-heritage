THE WATCH MUSEUM

   The Watch Museum, unique in Romania, was inaugurated in 1963 in a room of the Culture Palace in Pitesti. The museum exIsts thanks to professor Nicolae Simache, who was the director of the History Museum between 1954 - 1971. 
   The watch collection was moved later in a house built at the end of the 19th century. This house belonged to the former Prefect (Governor) of the Prahova district , Luca Elefterescu, a renown conservative politician who was active at the beginning of first decade of the 20th century. 
    The museum introduces its visitors to the domain of the evolution of means for the time measuring, from first sun watches to burning watches,  watches with water and with sand till the antiques and modern ones.
    The wonderful works are very often not only masterpieces of renown horlogical masters, but also of artists, who contributed to their attractive presentation and created schools and styles. 
    We display below several exhibits from the museum's collection, as shown on six postal stamps that appeared on 20th of May 1988 under the name "The Watch Museum Ploiesti". 

  • 50 B. Porcelain clock from Arad (20th century)
  • 1.50 L. French bronze clock (20th century)
  • 2 L. French bronze clock (19th century)
  • 3 L. Bronze gothic clock (19th century)
  • 4 L. Saxon porcelain clock (19th century)
  • 5 L. Porcelain clock from Bohemia (19th century)
