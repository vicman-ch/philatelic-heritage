Local Issues of Sighet
 

Jay T. Carrigan, USA

Sighet /Máramarossziget

County Maramureş / Máramaros

November 29, 1944

 
   
Overprinted by order of the postmaster with a handstamp carved by hand in wood.  According to the Sighet protocol, the handstamp was burned after overprinting.  Printed in black ink which generally bleeds through to the back of the stamp.  All stamps in greenish-black ink (blue-green from the back) are forgeries. The Sighet protocol mentions only nine stamps (7 issued stamps plus 2 trial printings) as being overprinted.  For many years, catalogs have been listing 13 stamps (10 plus 3 trials).
 

Circular overprint POSTA / 1944 / ROMÂNĂ

In genuine overprints, the left leg of the 
M of ROMÂNĂ is broken or missing

No covers or genuinely used examples have ever been found.  Some references claim that this issue was used on registered mail, while cash franking was employed for ordinary mail.  Contradicting this, registered letters from Sighet in December 1944 have been found which employ only cash franking.
