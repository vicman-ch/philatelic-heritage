ROMANIAN CURRENCY AS IT APPEARS ON ROMANIAN STAMPS

In the year 1868 a new currency was introduced on stamps, that replaced the older one, from Turkish language, Parale.

The name of this currency, one that survived till today, is LEU.

Singular: 1 leu (literally means "one lion"). Plural: 2 or more lei

The Leu was subdivided into 100 Bani, something that never changed since 1868.

Singular: 1 Ban
Plural: 2 or more Bani (bani means also "money")

1 leu = 100 bani

On the stamps, the name of leu (lei) can appear either fully, or abbreviated as L. The abbreviation for ban (bani) is b.

When Lei appears before the numeric value, it is correct to write: LEI 1 (like LEI 2).

It is also correct 1 + 1 LEI for surcharged stamps.

On the stamps issued in 1894 and later there are also values written as 1 BANI 1. This can be considered correct, because BANI 1 is correct (even is 1 BANI isn't, the correct form being 1 Ban).
