ABOUT ROMANIA

To Mãrioara Manta

This page displays some nice Romanian stamps, covering different aspects of the life in this quite special and often amazing country, situated in Europe, in the North of the Balkan Peninsula.

Issued in 1986 and commemorating the 50th anniversary of the Open Air Museum of Historic Dwelling in Bucharest. Scott 3387 - 3392

Issued in 1977. Romanian Male Folk Dancers - Calusari. Scott 2748 - 2753.

Issued in 1975. Romanian Peasant Rugs from Muntenia, Moldavia and Maramures. Scott 2583 - 2588.

Issued in 1985. Women's and men's costumes from same region printed in continuous design. From Muscel, Bistrita-Nasaud and Vrancea. Scott 3314-3321.

Issued in 1982. Romanian fair tales. Scott 3094 - 3099.

Issued in 1987. Pelicans over the Danube Delta, Transfagarasan High-way, Lake Capra, Mt. Fagaras, Orchard in Borsa. Scott 3452 - 3457.

Background: Ion Andreescu, Trees in Blossom, 1880?, The National Art Museum of Romania, Bucharest.

Frescoes Grigorescu Andreescu & Luchian Brancusi Shot Art Wildlife
