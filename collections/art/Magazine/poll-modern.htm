The Best Stamp

Vote closed, thank you!

  

You are invited to vote on some of nicest modern art on stamps. To vote, click on Cast your Vote found below, then just click the small white circles found at the top of the preferred stamp. After having voted, you can ask for the statistics of all votes. Press the "Back to site" link to return to this site, or use the menu to continue your visit of it. Please note that some publicity may appear, that isn't related to this site. Choose attentively from the proposed paintings, because the principle "one man, one vote" applies. If you like several paintings, choose all of them. Click on "View Stats" to view the statistics, without having to vote. Move  the mouse index over the painting in stats for displaying some information about the works of art.

You cannot vote from this page, it represents only a preview of what you will see on the voting page.
