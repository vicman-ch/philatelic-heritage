Beautiful Arts Stamps from The Netherlands

 The Netherlands. June 8, 1999. Paintings of Carel Fabritius, Judith Leyster, Jan Steen, Adriaen Coorte, Pieter Saenredam, Rembrandt (1 + 2), Hendrick Ter Brugghen, Gabriel Metsu, Jacob van Ruisdael.

The Netherlands. Nice maximum cards, full of humor, inspired by the works of Rembrandt (Self-portrait and Danae). Published by Capucine Mazille, Art Unlimited Postbus 1760 1000 BT Amsterdam. The whole set contains 8 maximum cards. Reproduced at about 30% of the original size, for information purposes.
