The Best Stamp

Vote closed, thank you!

  

You are invited to vote on some of worst art on stamps. To vote, just click in the field at bottom of the worst stamp and then on the Vote! button. After having voted, the statistics of all votes will be displayed in a separate window. Press the "Close Windows" button in order to close that window. Please note that some publicity may appear, that isn't related to this site. Choose attentively, because the principle "one man, one vote" applies. If you like several paintings, choose all of them. Click on "View Stats" to view the statistics, without having to vote. Put the mouse index over the painting in stats for displaying some information about the work of art.

You can find out more about the above stamps and about many others by visiting this site (just click the banner shown below).

Thanks a lot for your interest in Art on Stamps!
