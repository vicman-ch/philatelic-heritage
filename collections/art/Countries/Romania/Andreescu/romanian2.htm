 The painter Ion Andreescu was was born in the year 1850 in Bucharest and died only 32 years later in the same city. 

(For more information about the works of art displayed please point to the stamps with the mouse index.)

Like the painter Nicolae Grigorescu, he passed also through Barbizon.

STEFAN LUCHIAN

Stefan Luchian was born at Stefanesti (near Botosani) in 1868 and died, fully paralyzed, in Bucharest, in 1916. He was the painter of the most beautiful flowers ever in the Romanian art. See below some extracts of his wonderful work.
