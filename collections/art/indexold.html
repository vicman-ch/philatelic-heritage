The stamps mosaic is based on the painting "The Archduke William in His Gallery in Brussels", by David Teniers the Younger (1610-1690). This work of art is exhibited at the Historical Museum of Fine Arts, Vienna. On the left hand of the the archduke the author painted himself. Because many of  original paintings on the wall were gradually replaced by more famous ones, shown on stamps, the poor David finally lost his head and got another, more appropriate one. Should you temporarily miss on this painting the original head, then click the Refresh button of your browser.
