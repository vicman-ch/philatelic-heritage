Stamps of Lithuania

      The dictators and dictatorships around the world think that they will last forever. Usually they make propaganda on stamps for their "achievements", without to bother how will they be judged by future generations, and neglecting the fact that the dictatorships come and go, but the stamps remain, as precious evidences of the past.
      This is the chance for the stamp collectors who, being interested in history as reflected by the stamps of given countries, can find interesting relationships between the historical facts and the stamps of different periods.
        The Danish author and PASIC member, Mrs. Ann Mette Heindorff, proposes interesting philatelic pages about a small country, Lithuania, that experienced a long occupation and that liberated itself when it became possible. I find this pages very interesting for all stamp collector, but especially for readers for whom the greatest part of the covered events happened a long time ago, and for readers living in countries that are a long way off Lithuania and generally off Europe.
       If the author agrees, I will provide a link from my pages consecrated to Romanian postal history to the page about the Lithuanian stamps. Congratulations for the useful work and especially for the results! (after a posting, published on RCSD the 10/09/2000).
