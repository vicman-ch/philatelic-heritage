Spanish Regional Costumes

1967 - 1969

To My Spanish Heart 

   One of the most beautiful costumes sets was issued by Spain between 1967 and 1971. The set consist of a total of 53 values, Scott 1392 - 1444. My costumes collection started actually with this wonderful set. Not only the costumes are nice, but so are the models, presented in very natural and in quite different positions.

     1967. Regions: Alava, Albacete, Alicante, Almeria, Avila

    1967. Regions: Badajos, Baleares, Barcelona, Burgos, Cadiz

    1967 - 1968. Regions: Cordoba, Castellon de la Plana, Ciudad Real, Caceres, Coruna

     1968. Regions: Cuenca, Fernando Po, Gerona, Grenada, Guadalajara 

     1968 - 1969. Regions: Guipuzcoa, Huelva, Huesca, Ifni, Jaen
