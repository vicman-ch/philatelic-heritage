Spanish Regional Costumes

1969 - 1970

To My Spanish Heart 

   Here is the continuation of the set of costumes that was issued by Spain between 1967 and 1971, a total of 53 values, Scott 1392-1444. 

     1969. Regions: Jaen, Las Palmas, Leon, Lerida, Logroso

    1969. Regions: Lugo, Madrid, Malaga, Muercia, Navarra

    1969 - 1970. Regions: Orense, Oviedo, Palencia, Ponteverda, Sahara

     1970. Regions: Salamanca, Sta. Cruz de Tenerife, Santander, Segovia, Sevilla

    I recommend to anybody to try to find this nice and quite inexpensive set and to start with it a nice costume collection, like the presented one.
