    Some of author's choices are not obvious. One can wonder why are all Romanian (as the single European country on the list) sheets since 1972 placed in this select company, especially because in the last years the issuing politic of Romanian Post has changed. This could be understandable for certain Romanian imperforated sheets of the 70s and 80s, that were sold only abroad, but other sheets are not better or worse than those of some other European countries. Romania has issued between 1972 and 1991 about 175 sheets (of course much too many), but please note that USSR has issued in the same period of time about 135 sheets, what is not much less either. The size of the country is nor relevant for this issue, because USSR had print runs for its sheets that were considerably bigger that those of Rumanian ones, quantities that were roughly adapted to its bigger population and to the number of collectors of its stamps. Or even better, take Hungary, that issued during the same period of time also about 135 sheets, most of them also as imperforated (i.e. a total of over 250 different sheets). Nevertheless we have published the list as such.

    This list is anyway very useful, especially for young and/or inexperienced stamp collectors. Thanks a lot, Mr. Bengt Bengtsson!

    The source for the list: Swiss Stamps Magazine, SBZ 3/2001, p.159.
