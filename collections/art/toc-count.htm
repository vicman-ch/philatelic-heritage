COUNTRIES OF THE WORLD

 

    The pages with the subject Countries show the  achievements in arts of masters of different countries and cultures, as reflected on the postal stamps of the respective territories and of other territorial units.

    This pages will not be strictly limited  to art, but will also try to show images that describe the history, the art of living and the customs of each particular country that will be dealt with.

    Because this approach transgresses the main purpose of the present site, the non-artistical pages will be displayed at the end of the presentation, separately for each country, giving to the guest the choice about going beyond the explicit goal of this site.
