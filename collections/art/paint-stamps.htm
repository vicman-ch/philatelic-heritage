PAINTINGS STAMPS WORLDWIDE

... The Web Site for Paintings and Arts on Stamps. Since 1996 ....

This is a site dedicated to Paintings Stamps worldwide. It was created in 1996 and was then the first site dedicated to the topic Paintings on Stamps. It has continually grown to the most complete and multilateral site dedicated to the topic Paintings Stamps on the Internet. Some interesting visual information (images of stamps featuring paintings), combined with written information about the stamps and the paintings that are displayed is waiting for you. I wish you a lot of fun - and if you have children, please show them some pages - I'm quite sure that children will love the stamps!  Please choose your language in order to enter the first page of the Paintings Stamps site, written in your language. Yours Web & StampsMaster.
