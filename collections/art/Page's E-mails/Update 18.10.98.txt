The most important news concerning the Paintings and Arts on Stamps Site is
that its URL has changed. I have got a Domain Name, therefore the URL will
remain stable in the predictable future. The new URL is: http://www.values.ch. Please Bookmark
(put in Favorites) this new URL.

In the last months the site was updated several times. Some important
changes:

- Under the main Table of Contents you can find some new pages dedicated to
classical stamps (London 90 exhibition), to errors on stamps and a Quiz
(some stamps wait to be won there). The new issues information pages are
up-to-date too.

- In the Art Gallery there are some new entries, concerning Switzerland,
Music, Sailing and Stained-Glass Art. Some earlier pages were updated too.

- Under My Column, some pages were continually updated (My Mail, Postings)
and there are two new pages: Rights and December.

- The Paintings and Arts Database was also updated (over 4000 records, 15000 stamps, 1500 S/S). Free download.

Some new JavaScript code helps to a better navigation.

I hope that you will enjoy the site and I wish you all a happy browsing!

Victor Manta, in Switzerland
