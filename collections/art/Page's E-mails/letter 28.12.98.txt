On December 23rd 1998 the multilingual  "Paintings and Arts on Stamps" site will celebrate its second anniversary. It was initially thought as a mean to offer to stamps collectors an useful information about the stamps belonging to that topic (by a news announcements section and a free downloadable database, that lists today over 15000 stamps and over 1500 souvenir sheets) and a free advertisements section. Then it was extended gradually, an Art Gallery was added, presenting some great artists (Vermeer, Raphael, Van Gogh, Hiroshige, ..) and a selection of interesting subjects (Music, Sailing, Love, Nudes, Stained-glass, Pottery, ...) on its 25 pages. Other new pages are related to subjects of more general interest, like stamps exhibitions (London 90), the Universal Postal Union, or poetry and stamps. The My Column section treats some controversial topics related to stamps issue politics, values of stamps, stamps without a face value and some more general political and philosophical subjects.

The Paintings on Stamps Internet Club (free membership), created in 1997, that has at time 49 members from 26 different countries, facilitated the contacts and even created friendship relations among its members.

I would like to thank the nearly 7000 visitors of the "Paintings and Arts on Stamps" site, for their fidelity and their encouragements and wish them and all stamps collectors worldwide: Happy Holidays, Frohe Festtage, Bonnes Fêtes, Sarbatori Fericite!

Victor Manta, Web Publisher
