******** A message from the President *************

Dear PASIC members and stamps collectors,

Even if you haven't heard for a long time something new from me, it doesn't imply that  in the meantime nothing happened. The contrary is actually true and I have the pleasure to inform you about some interesting developments.

First of all, considering that PASIC has grown and counts at time 71 members from 32 countries, we decided to give a structure to our club and to transform it in a formal organization. Consequently the bylaws were rewritten and published in four languages on the Paintings and Arts on Stamps (PAS) site. According to the Art. 12, PASIC is led by its President and a staff of Officers that compose the Executive Council. The Officers are co-opted among the members of PASIC by the President. The Executive Council is composed of a Vice-President, a Secretary, a Treasurer, an Editor and two Directors. The Statutes and the list of Officers were published on the pages: http://www.values.ch\PASIC\statutes-en.htm, ...\statutes-fr.htm,...\statutes-gr.htm and ...\statutes-ro.htm.

We decided recently to publish on the PAS site a Club Journal. You can consult its first edition at:  http://www.values.ch\PASIC\journal.htm. Every member of the club is invited to contribute to our journal with interesting news and/or articles.

The PAS site ( http://www.values.ch) itself was considerably expanded this year. Several older pages were enhanced and re-formatted for a better compatibility with different screen resolutions and for more appeal. An important number of new pages was created, covering such different subject as Landscapes, Formosa, Gauguin or Hans Erni (a Swiss artist). A new Magazine suite was designed and published. This site starts with an article dedicated to the most famous philatelic items worldwide. Another new suite was named Champions and is dedicated to true champions from different domains of activity. As usual, it is illustrated with some nice stamp images.

Thanks also to some PASIC members (especially to our Vice-President Mrs. Helen Fowler from the USA, whom I'd like to thank here again), the free downloadable database from the PAS site has grown considerably. The Members List is stored in an Access database on the server and some new JavaScript code permit to new members to input data relevant data to them on-line.

Some of our members were also active. I recommend you to visit the site "Czech and Slovak Stamps Artists" at:  http://home.t-online.de/home/batz.hausen/stampart.htm
published by the PASIC member and Officer, Mr. Gerhard Batz and the new site "Travelling the World on Stamps", at: http://w1.1429.telia.com/~u142900356/ of our member and Officer Mrs. Ann Mette Heindorff.  

Last, but not least, I would like to thank to all PASIC members and to all visitors of PAS site for their interest, fidelity and encouragements and I wish you all "A Happy Stamps Collecting"! 

Victor Manta

PASIC President
