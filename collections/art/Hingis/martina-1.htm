In Fielderstadt Martina has won her 4th Porsche and also the 32nd final in her career. Together with Anna Kournikova she also won in double, against the pair Sanchez-Vicario/Schett, by 2:0. In Zurich Martina, named also the Swiss Miss, won this year for the first time an ATP tournament in Switzerland, and Davenport lost her first tournament as a professional player in the same country. Together with Anna Kournikova, Martina also won in double the final game in Zurich. Hingis won in the year 77 from a total of 87 games. Only 2 games were lost in 1/4 finals, all others in semi-finals or finals. On Nov. 19, 2000, Martina won the Masters Tournament in New York, at Madison Square Garden, in a dramatic game against a great Monica Seles. Together with Anna Kournikova she won also the double tournament. The year 2000 was a great year for Martina, the best female tennis player in the world. Congratulations!
