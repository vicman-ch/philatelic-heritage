The stamps mosaic is based on the painting The Archduke William in His Gallery in Brussels, by David Teniers the Younger (1610-1690), exhibited at the Historical Museum of Fine Arts, Vienna. The images for the entry points were taken from the  painting From Maroco, webmaster's property, a work of the Swiss artist Heinz Grüter.
