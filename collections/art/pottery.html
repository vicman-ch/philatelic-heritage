Pottery and Ceramics on Stamps

A selection of original postage stamps celebrating pottery, porcelain and ceramic art, restored from the historical Art on Stamps collection.

Pottery is made from clay hardened by firing in a kiln. Its characteristics depend on the clay, preparation, firing temperature and glaze. Unlike porcelain, traditional pottery is generally porous and opaque.
