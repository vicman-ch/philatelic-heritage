VISITING PWO FORUM FOR THE FIRST TIME, AND REGISTRATION.

The easiest way for entering in our PWO Forum is by clicking (once) on "Forum" ...

Choose a user name and a password (and write them somewhere, if you've to many other passwords to remember). Your user name will be shown on all your forum messages, so please try to let it be understandable for other members (for example Victor Manta is "manvic", and André Hurtré is "Andre"). Your password should  remain confidential.

Overwrite "Gast" with your user name, and enter your password. You may also select "Save my login info for next time" (you will see later how much will be saved - normally only the password).

As far as we know software makes not difference between caps and lower cases letters.

Both your user name and password are working either from an English page or from a French one. But they are valid only in the Forum zone.

A new window ("Please Register") will be opened, after having clicked on "REGISTER". Please add the required information ("Username" and "Password" must be the same ones that those you've entered in the previous window).

Clicking on "REGISTER" will register you as a life time member of our Forum. Please select (from left menu) "APWP" for the PWO Forum. You're welcomed on board !

VISITING AGAIN

If you want visit again your PWO Forum, you will have to enter your Username and your Password (the ones you've written somewhere ... you know where) and to click on "EINLOGGEN".

In the new window, don't forget to select "APWP". Other buttons seems to be fully understandable for our English speaking members

Use "PRIVATE MESSAGE" only when you are sure that your addressee expects to get private post.
