Undesirable / Abusive Stamps

Mr. Bengt Bengtsson, a philatelic judge from Sweden, prepared a classification of "undesirable stamps" (3/2001) that he felt should not appear in collections presented at philatelic exhibitions. We updated this list by adding some further countries, based on what we learned when observing the philatelic market.
