Illegal Stamp Issues from Kyrgyzstan

Kyrgyzstan – Illegal Issues of Postage Stamps

UNIVERSAL

POSTAL UNION

Berne, 5 May 2003

International Bureau Circular

172

Dear Sir/Madam

The postal administration of KYRGYZSTAN asks me to inform you of the following:

"The postal administration of Kyrgyzstan has information about illegal issues of postage stamps bearing the symbol of Kyrgyzstan.

"The following issues:

–    the miniature sheet issued in 2002, comprising nine (3x3) "HARRY POTTER" stamps with a face value of three times 10 KGS, three times 20 KGS and three times 30 KGS;

–    the two miniature sheets issued in 2002, comprising nine (3x3) "MERRY CHRISTMAS" (cartoon heroes) stamps with a face value of three times 10 KGS, three times 20 KGS and three times 30 KGS;

–    the miniature sheet issued in 2002, comprising nine (3x3) "SHAHMATY" stamps with a face value of 20 KGS;

–    the block of stamps issued in 2001, comprising three "JOHN PAUL II" stamps with a face value of 10 KGS, 20 KGS and 30 KGS,

were published in contravention of the Universal Postal Convention and Kyrgyz legislation. Consequently, since these stamps were not issued by the postal administration of Kyrgyzstan, they may not be used as a means of postal prepayment ".

Yours faithfully,

K J S McKEOWN

Director of Communication and Postal Markets

308

Dear Sir/Madam

The postal administration of KYRGYZSTAN asks me to inform you of the following:

"The postal administration of Kyrgyzstan has learned that illegal issues of postage stamps with the symbol of its State are still being advertised on a website. We should therefore be grateful if you would inform Union member countries of the following.

"The following stamp series:

● series of 9 stamps "Backstreet Boys", face value 20 s;

● series of 6 stamps "Jennifer Aniston", face value 20 s;

● series of 6 stamps "Tennis", face value 20 s;

● series of 9 stamps "Beverly Hills 90210", face value 20 s;

● series of 9 stamps "Penguins", face value 20 s;

● series of 9 stamps "Tom and Jerry", face value 20 s;

● series of 9 stamps "Prince William", face value 20 s;

● series of 9 stamps "Babylon 5", face value 20 s;

● series of 9 stamps "End of Mir", face value 20 s

were issued in violation of the Detailed Regulations of the UPU Convention and the legislation of the Republic of Kyrgyzstan. They can not therefore be accepted as a means of postal prepayment."

Yours faithfully,

M MAZOU

UNIVERSAL

POSTAL UNION

Berne, 10 July 2000

International Bureau Circular

258

Dear Sir/Madam

The postal administration of KYRGYZSTAN asks me to inform you that it has received information about illegal issues of postage stamps in its name.

These stamps have the symbol of the State printed on them, just like official issues. They show motifs representing Western show business personalities and have been reproduced in a Japanese periodical.

All "Beatles" and "Madonna" stamps with a face value of 20 s have therefore been issued in contravention of the provisions of the Detailed Regulations of the Universal Postal Convention and the laws of the Republic of Kyrgyzstan.

As the postal administration of Kyrgyzstan has never issued such postage stamps, they cannot be accepted as a means of postage.

Yours faithfully,

M S RAMAN

 

UNIVERSAL

POSTAL UNION

Berne, 13 December 1999

International Bureau Circular

432

Dear Sir/Madam

The postal administration of KYRGYZSTAN asks me to inform you about the illegal issue of postage stamps relating to its State.

The following stamps have been issued in contravention of the Detailed Regulations of the Universal Postal Convention and the legislation of the Kyrgyz Republic:

  • "Chess" set, 10, 15, 20 soms;
  • "71st Academicians' annual award ceremony" set, 20 soms;
  • "Playthings" set, 15 soms;
  • "Cats" set, 100, 200, 400, 800, 1000, 1600, 2000, 24 100 tiyns;
  • "Princess Diana" stamps and blocks, 1000, 5000 tiyns;
  • "Bruce Willis" stamps, 10 soms.

In view of the above, these stamps are to be regarded as illegal and may not be used for the prepayment of postage.

Yours faithfully,

M S RAMAN
Assistant Director-General

More information about the illegal issues can be obtained directly from the UPU, Mrs. Maria Libera.
