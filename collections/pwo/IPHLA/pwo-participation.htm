PWO MEMBERS PARTICIPATION
IN IPHLA 2012 WEB SITES COMPETITION

Between Nov. 2 and Nov. 4, 2012 the Gutenberg city of Mainz, Germany, hosted the most important International Philatelic Literature Exhibition of the last decades, the IPhLA 2012.

Eleven members of the PWO participated in this exhibition with their sites, very successfully. We congratulate all of them for their excellent results. Here is the list of our participants and of their exhibits:
