Our Reasons and Goals

The Association of Philatelic Web Publishers (APWP) has been created in order to promote, support and develop philately through publishing activity on the Web.

There are many national and international organizations that group the stamp collectors or the philatelic professionals representing their interests. At the moment there is no organization grouping the recently appeared category of stamp collectors who combine their philatelic hobby with the Web publishing activity. On these pages such stamp collectors are called the "Web Publishers" (WP). 

The WP are quite different from the philatelic journalists, meaning here those who work for philatelic journals and who are grouped in the International Association of Philatelic Journalists (AIJP). The main difference is that most of WP are amateurs. They bring to the Net what they love so much: their stamps and information about them and their knowledge about Web publishing. All of them started by themselves, being pushed by the impulse to share with others what they like, and by their interest to explore a new dimension of the human communication, made possible by the technological (r)evolution.

For many of WP the newsgroup rec.collecting.stamps.discuss was till now the only place where they could discuss their problems, or where they could announce their new pages. But they had and still have to take in consideration the interests of the majority of participants, interests that aren't focused on Web publishing.

Some of us, the most advanced and/or the most brave, have participated at Web competitions proposed by organizations representing the traditional philately (like FIP, MIDAPHIL). We were often disappointed by the juries' lack of knowledge and/or lack of understanding of the specificity of Web publishing. The noting criteria were not clear or absent, the qualification of judges in Web publishing was unknown and the promised score sheets never arrived.

In the last years the number of young stamp collectors has continually diminished. New possibilities, like exciting games, computers and Internet became more attractive than stamp collecting. Through the Web publishing activity in the philatelic domain, we have a new chance to regain the young generations' interest for stamp collecting.

For all these reasons the time has come that we, the Web Publishers, take our destiny in our own hands! The best mean to solve our own problems and to attain our goals is to act together, as members of a structured organization. These are the reasons why we have established the Association of Philatelic Web Publishers (APWP). Our big advantage is that we have the means and the knowledge to efficiently communicate worldwide, with a matchless efficiency and at minimal costs.

What can an APWP member expect from his organization and from his colleagues:

  • He (and she) is listed on the Members List, together with many others who share his interests. This not only makes his site and himself better visible, but also presents him in the proper context of sites that have many characteristics in common. 
  • As member he will get listed the name of his site, the topic (or  the specialization for commercial sites), the kind of site (Homepage, Commercial, Postal Authority, Organization), the URL of his site, his name as the author of his site, the language, the country where he lives and his e-mail address.
  • The membership facilitates the relationship and the exchange of experience between him and other members, thus encouraging direct contacts.
  • The APWP organizes on-line study groups, meetings, forums,  common exhibitions and other activities.
  • Periodically the APWP proposes different kinds of Web sites competitions (worldwide, regional, thematic, etc.), for different levels, judged by juries who are competent in their field. The judges use clearly established and widely known criteria.
  • A member has the possibility to announce the results of his work (new sites or pages) on a specialized Forum and to publish pages of general interest on the APWP site.
  • Through the interaction with many other members who share his interests, he can take profit as much from the technical expertise as from the artistic and philatelic experience of all members.
  • Member's privacy is protected by the APWP Statutes.

Some of the proposed facilities, like the on-line joining of the APWP and the free listing of all members on a Web site, are offered from the very beginning. The totality of the stated  objectives will be attained gradually, just in time when a certain critical mass of members has been reached.

And last, but not least, the success  of the APWP depends of the activity of us, its members. The results depend of our imagination, initiative and spirit of collaboration. Together we will succeed!

Victor Manta
Founder, Feb. 2001
