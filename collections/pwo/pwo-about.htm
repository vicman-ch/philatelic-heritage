The Philatelic Webmasters Organization — background, contact information and historical resources concerning prohibited stamp issues.

About

The Philatelic Webmasters Organization (PWO) has been created in 2001 to promote, support and develop philately through philatelic webmastering. Its site fights against the proliferation of illegal and excessive stamps.
