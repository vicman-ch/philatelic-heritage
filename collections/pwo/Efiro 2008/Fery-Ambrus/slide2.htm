Postal stamps on fiscal use

Francisc Ambruş, Bucureşti, România

 On the presented postcards and letters, some fiscal stamps was used replaced the postal stamps or for complete the postal taxes. Could somebody have confused the taxes and stamps which should have been applied? In this case the legal postal tax fees were not respected.


Questions which request an answer. Without doubts you can affirm that this pieces is unusual, worthy of being presented both in postage stamp and revenue stamp catalogues.

I make an appeal to collectors to express a point of view on this piece and to present the eventual similar pieces which they know or have in their collections.
