Local Issues of Satu Mare

Satu Mare / Szatmárnémeti

County Satu Mare / Szatmár

Early 1945

 
   
The Jewish population of Satu Mare was 12,960 in 1941.  The Horthy administration at first sent Jews to forced labor camps, but by 1944 the entire Jewish population had been sent to Auschwitz.  Only a few hundred survived the war.  To raise funds to aid the returning refugees, the municipal council of Satu Mare issued two revenue stamps to be used on legal documents.  The stamps were perforated through the middle so that on half could be attached to the legal document and the other to a receipt.  The bilingual text on the stamps translates to "For the Deported".  Quantities printed are not known.
