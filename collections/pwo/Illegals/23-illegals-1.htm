ILLEGAL STAMPS JANUARY - MARS 2023

Victor Manta
Philatelic journalist, PWO (*)

 

So far this year, we have identified 34 countries in whose names illegal “stamps” have been produced including the year 2023 (A summary list of the first few countries can be found at the end of the message. Included are the type of topics that are reproduced). And we have just completed only three months of this year. Unfortunately, the majority of these countries are on the African continent. Those from other regions are Guyana, Haiti, Lao, Maldives and Solomon Islands.

Quite a few countries have variations in the way that the country name is reproduced and "stamps" on the same topics are produced in the various ways a country;s name is reproduced. At least two countries have their names obviously misspelled. A further attack on the country's sovereignty is the inclusion of the term “POSTES” as part of the illegal “stamp”. To be noted that more countries are victims of illegal “stamps” in addition to those already identified in the list below that include the year 2023 – for example, for 2022 and earlier, such as Angola, Eritrea, Malawi, Nigeria, Tchad, Togo, among others.

An initial summary of the countries concerned by the production in their names of illegal “stamps” so far for 2023.
