Illegal Stamp Issues from Niger

NIGER - Illegal Issues of Postage Stamps

UNIVERSAL

POSTAL UNION

Berne, 5 June 2000

International Bureau Circular

194

Dear Sir/Madam

The postal administration of NIGER asks me to inform you of the following:

"I enclose a list of stamps issued without the authorization of Niger Post by a French company.

"I wish to inform you that my administration brought an action. about the fraudulent activities of this individual before the Regional Court of Niamey which, at a hearing on 19 October 1999, imposed a six-month suspended prison sentence and a fine on the manager.

"Another action is being brought before the Paris courts, to be heard on 4 July 2000 in the 31st Chambre correctionnelle."

Yours faithfully,

E M GHARBI
Assistant Director-General
