2003 — 8 circulars

Guinea (Rep)

8 December 2003 – n° 421 · Entry 8

Guinea Post Office condemns a considerable number of illegal postage stamps which have been produced and freely disseminated on the philatelic market by private companies and dealers on the following subjects : - "Celebrities/famous figures" - Immoral scenes, sexually explicit and completely unethical (five miniature sheets in the same vertical format, each containing four postage stamps with the inscription "Pin-Up" in English (various half clad women) as well as the actress Marilyn. The "Scouts" logo is also included. - "Fairy tales, fantastic tales, cartoons" - Ten miniature sheets, each depicting a scene from a different fairy tale in the background, with a series of five postage stamps. At the top of each miniature sheet, in the middle, there is the inscription "Les contes populaires du monde" ("Folk tales of the world"). - "Paintings" - Fifteen miniature sheets with the inscription "American Impressionists" in English, each depicting a painting by the artist con- cerned in the background, with a series of four postage stamps. The "Rotary International" logo is also included. - "Vehicles (Transport)" - Trains and locomotives - Nineteen miniature sheets, each with a series of four postage stamps with the inscription "Les trains legendaries du monde" ("Legendary trains of the world"). The "Rotary International" logo is also included.

Eritrea

25 August – n°294 · Entry 6

Illegal postage stamp issues produced mostly as sheetlets and miniature sheets in the name of Eritrea on the following thematics. Many stamps carry the emblem/logo of the Rotary and Scouts organisations. Animals: different species of bears, bison or buffalo, crocodiles, dinosaurs, dogs, elephants, frogs, horses, monkeys, pandas, snakes, tigers, turtles, wild jungle cats - Birds: different species of birds, birds of prey, fowl, parrots, penguins - Cartoons: "The Beatles Yellow Submarine") - Events: "Tribute to 11th Sept. Firefighters" - Fish species/Marine creatures - Flora: flowers (orchid), mushrooms - Insects: bees, butterflies, spiders – Lighthouses - Paintings: "The Impressionists" (Camille Corot, Camille Pissarro, Pierre Auguste Renoir), "Vincent van Gogh", “Edward Hopper”, “Jack Vettriano” - People/Personalities (including actors, actresses, singers, etc.): Brigitte Bardot, Elvis Presley, Grace Kelly, Marilyn Monroe, Sophia Loren - Immoral and Provocative Scenes (sexually explicit in some cases): sheetlet of nine depicting young women together on a golf course, sheetlets of nine each inscribed "SEXY ACTRESSES", "SEXY MODELS", "PIN UP" ("The Art of Elvgren"), "BORIS VALLEJO", "DORIAN CLEAVENGER", "Michael Möbius", "OLIVIA" and "Ricky Carralero". - Teddy Bears: sheetlets and miniature sheets depicting “Mole Island Bears" - Vehicles – Transportation: cars, Concorde, motorcycles/motor racing, trains/locomotives The Rotary emblem and the Scouts logo appear on many of the illegal stamps. Different websites carry different prices.

Côte d’Ivoire

28 July – n°269 · Entry 5

Illegal postage stamp issues produced mostly as sheetlets and miniature sheets in the name of the Republic of Côte d’Ivoire on the following thematics: groups of shells, different dog breeds, species of butterfly, orchids, the actor Elvis Presley, the actress Marilyn Monroe, motorbike races, table tennis players, vintage fire engines and different trains. Many stamps carry the emblem of a specific organization. A certain number of miniature sheets are listed in dollars on one website at a very high price (19.95 USD) for the perforated format for the two sheets and at double the price (39.80 USD) for the imperforated format. The same miniature sheets are listed on another website at a much cheaper price (e.g. 3.00 USD each).

Kyrgyzstan

5 May - n°172 · Entry 4

Illegal issues of postage stamps: sheetlets issued in 2002, comprising nine (3x3) stamps with face values of 10 KGS, 20 KGS and 30 KGS depicting "HARRY POTTER", "MERRY CHRISTMAS" (cartoon heroes) stamps, "JOHN PAUL II" and "SHAHMATY" (chess) stamps ; a sheet of stamps issued in 2001 depicting "JOHN PAUL II".

Mauritania

28 April – n° 161 · Entry 3

Illegal postage stamp issues produced in the name of the Islamic Republic of Mauritania which include numerous souvenir sheets and sheetlets, sold in both the imperforate and perforate format portraying different type of bird (including birds of prey, ducks, pheasants, field birds and sea birds), different species of owl, various penguin scenes, various cats playing together, various elephant and polar bear scenes, different lighthouses, various types of firearm, various sports of the Sydney Olympics, various other sports (tennis, horse- riding, swimming, volleyball, table tennis, fencing, judo and cycling), different trains, different portraits of Pope John Paul II, the singer Elvis Presley, the actress Marilyn Monroe, The Beatles, Walt Disney, classic actresses, different scenes from the Harry Potter films, the group Queen, different scenes from the television series "The Simpsons", "TEDDY BEARS", "Scooby Doo". The value shown on each stamp is in UM and each stamp is inscribed with the year 2002. A number of the stamps feature the International Rotary emblem, while on others there is the Scout emblem.
