Members Gallery

    We at the Philatelic Webmasters Organization have found that an interesting mean to promote philately on the Web is to give our members the possibility to use their PWO site in order to show some of their most  attractive and most representative pages. In the same time the PWO members get the opportunity to present themselves, through an introductory page. We hope that you will like that what our members have to show. Please visit the PWO Gallery of pages.

    Victor Manta, Switzerland. Links to Arts on Stamp pages, related the first one to Love and the second one to recent events in the tormented Balkans. United Nation PA cancellations in Geneva is the topic of his third page.
    Frank Daems, Belgium. Links to Canada Stamp Combinations, Channel Islands and Isle Of Man stamp margin imprints and European transport on rails related issues.
    André Hurtré, France. Links to a listing of all articles published by the French "Académie de Philatélie", a general page about international reply coupons, and information about French aerograms (postal stationery).
    Gerhard Batz, Germany. Links to pages about Czech and Slovak art and artists.
 
    Toon Oomens, The Netherlands. A summary of the stamps related to the Province of Zeeland, a short review of the Dutch Nobel Price Winners (in Dutch) and an illustration of author's activities in his youth club .
 
     Daniel Roudeillac, France. Links to some representative pages taken from the "Stamps and Defense" site, all of them following both the topical and the geopolitical approaches. 
 
    Lars Engelbrecht, Denmark. Danish postal stationery - the "bi-coloured" issues and their identification.

   The PWO members are kindly invited to submit by e-mail links to their member's pages to the PWO. They are asked to click here for more information.

   The visitors are invited to let us know, also by e-mail, what they think about the pages that we show, and also about our organization. The most pertinent mailings will be published on this site, without naming the authors. Thanks in advance!
