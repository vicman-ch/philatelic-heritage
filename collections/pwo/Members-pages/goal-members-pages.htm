The Goal of Members Pages

     The general goal of the Philatelic Webmasters Organization and of its members is to promote philately worldwide. That's why it is in the interest of the PWO to promote at best the creative work of its members, i.e. their own sites. One of the best means to attain this goal is to promote attractive, interesting pages of our members, connected through links to their own sites. These pages publish also a short information about the webmasters who developed them.

     That's why we intend to publish on the PWO site a page of links toward the representative pages of our members. Later, after the number of our members has grown, we will include these links into the database of our members and we will probably publish on this page of links mainly links to pages awarded through contests (please read below about future contests). 

    Your pages will be published on your own sites; you are asked to provide us only functioning links to them. It is up to you to include or not these pages into your own sites. By advertising them through the PWO, it is implicit that also the PWO has copyright to the published pages, and can use them for the purposes mentioned immediately hereafter. The PWO will use that right only for making publicity for the philatelic webmastering and for its members. This publicity will be made on the Web, through the PWO site, and maybe later through CD-ROMs made by the PWO, that could be presented at philatelic events as publicity for the PWO and for its members. The owners of such pages will in time be asked if they object to such publication. Any commercial use of Members Pages by the PWO or by others is explicitly prohibited.

    A contest of PWO Members Pages will be announced at a later time, with awards to be won. We will start with pages and not with sites simply because it is easier. This will bring to our interested members a practical experience in judging the work of their colleagues in philatelic webmastering. The awarding rules will be proposed on this site and will be discussed in the PWO Forum. The judges will be all PWO members who will express their wish to participate in this interesting challenge.

Indications for the presentation of Members Pages

    In order to attain our objectives, we ask the webmasters to maintain an uniform structure of Members Pages, as explained below. That means:

  • Start with the name of our organization. File to be used: www.pwmo.org/ttl-pwo.gif
  • Your name, as title
  • A short, but relevant presentation of yourself
  • Link(s) to my PWO member's page(s):  (Note. Limited to max. 5 pages)
  • At the bottom of the page please include (mm = month, dd = day, yy = year):

Created: mm/dd/01. 
Revised: mm/dd/yy.
Copyright © 2001 by YOUR NAME, Your Country.
All rights reserved worldwide.

You can link YOUR NAME either to your site (if you haven't a banner) or to your e-mail address.

The banner of your site (if you have one), linked to your site.

The PWO logo, found on the right and also callable from: www.pwmo.org/images/pwo-logo.gif . Please link it to: www.pwmo.org

    As already mentioned, you can use as an example of formatting my own page, published for this purpose on the PWO site. It will be moved later to my own site. Please click here to load it in a separate window.

   The PWO members are kindly invited to submit by e-mail links to their pages to the PWO.
