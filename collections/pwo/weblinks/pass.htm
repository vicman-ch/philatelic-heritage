PWO EDITORS ENTRY

     PWO site has recently integrated an important philatelic Web resource. Named PWO Links Online, it provides access to Web pages and other Internet resources related to stamp collecting, marketing, publishing and others. It is unique in providing the following advantages over other sites that provide philatelic Web links: 

  • Over 1900 links and constantly growing
  • Essentially no duplicate links
  • A live, fully searchable database of links instead of static out-of-date lists
  • The ability to view only new links
  • Close to 100% working links thanks to regular removal of dead links.

    Anyone can access our new resource through the menu point: PWO Links Online.

     We are searching among our members for PWO Editors, i.e. philatelic webmasters who are interested in developing the  excellent and unique philatelic resource named PWO Links Online. The PWO Editors have  the possibility to enhance (update) the present database. They enter online data concerning philatelic Web sites that aren't present in the database and that they judge to be worth of being made known.

     We hope that through a collective effort we will make this valuable resource even more complete and more useful for the Web philatelic world. 

    For applying as PWO Editor, and/or for more information, the PWO members are asked to contact their organization by e-mail. The approved Editors will then receive the Username and Password, and also more direction, and will be able to access the Editor's Domain.
