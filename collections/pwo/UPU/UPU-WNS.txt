Lisa Salcedo Pfeiffer has been working at the UPU since 2015, where she began as a support with the Philately programme, with particular focus on the world numbering system (WNS) for postage stamps. She now works as the programme's WNS specialist. The WNS was created and developed by  the  World  Association  for  the  Development  of  Philately  (WADP)  and the UPU in 2002 to combat illegal stamp issues. It not only serves as a  database  of  all  authentic  stamps  issued by UPU member countries, but also a directory for philatelists keen on accessing the world’s thousands of unique stamps. (...)

Aside from her WNS registration duties, Lisa says she is also working to modernize the system to give it better visibility, as well as improving its use as a means of combating illegal issues, as a source of expertise and as a  sales  platform. (...)

In coordination with the manager of  the  Philately  and  International  Reply Coupons programme, Lisa has full  responsibility  for  managing  the  WNS.

 The sad reality is that the very expensive WNS has not reduced the number of illegal stamps, which was and is obvious on the philatelic markets.

The most amazing is the fact that NO Illegal Stamps Circulars of the IB of the UPU were published in 2018 and 2019, something that never happened before!

http://www.wnsstamps.post/en/circulars
http://www.pwmo.org/Illegals/18-UPU-Interview-EN.htm

We should note that the lack of a UPU circular denouncing illegal stamps does not necessarily mean that there is a decrease in the number of illegal stamps or victim countries. It is common knowledge that many developing countries just do not have easy Internet access to do the market research to find out about illegal stamps. Many count on the goodwill of philatelists and stamp collectors to ask them directly about the legitimacy of their stamps.

Note also how the responsibility of checking information presented by the complaining postal administrations (P.A.) was shifted from the IB of the UPU, which used to perform preliminary research, to the P.A. themselves. It is not surprising that the number of denunciation of illegal stamps, as reflected by the UPU IB Circulars, has diminished. (2012)

http://www.pwmo.org/Illegals/WADP-WNS-Ballance-Sheet.htm


    The IB of the UPU doesn't research anymore the domain of illegal stamps. The explanation given by the UPU is that an alleged sharp decline in the production of these stamps occurred in the last years. Interestingly enough, its experts assert by themselves that their reference is "a number extracted from nowhere"
    Because the domain of illegals is not researched anymore by the UPU, its "experts" are not able to answer simple questions about the legitimacy of newer illegal issues. Answers like "It [Chad PA - VM] never replied to our demand and therefore we can consider that it is not an illegal issue because never denounced by this country" are obviously ridiculous.
    For the same reason the UPU stopped in 2017 the publication of its Circulars referring to illegal stamps. Please note that in the past the denunciations of illegals by the affected postal administrations (PA) were mostly based of the researches made by the UPU, for the reason that those rather disadvantaged PA were unable to make such researches by themselves.
