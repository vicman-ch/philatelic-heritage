About the archive

The Author — Victor Manta

This archive brings together five websites created and developed by Victor Manta, documenting a sustained contribution to philatelic research and the early culture of philately on the web.

Long-term preservation

The goal is to safeguard the original pages, illustrations and reference material as independently accessible collections. The preserved editions reproduce the supplied website files; some legacy dynamic services and external links may no longer function.

Preserving Our Philatelic Heritage

Philatelic Heritage is a digital preservation initiative created by Victor Manta to safeguard five historical philatelic websites developed over many years.

These websites document the relationship between postage stamps and art, history, politics, geography, and international philatelic cooperation. They also reflect an important chapter in the early development of philately on the Internet.

The purpose of this archive is not to rewrite the past, but to preserve it. Original articles, illustrations, and historical perspectives are maintained as part of the documentary value of these collections.

By making these resources freely accessible, Philatelic Heritage hopes to serve collectors, researchers, historians, and future generations interested in the cultural significance of postage stamps.
