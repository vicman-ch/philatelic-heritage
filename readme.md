# Victor Manta | Philatelic Heritage Archive

Welcome to the preservation repository of my lifetime of philatelic digital work. This archive preserves five independent, historical philatelic websites to ensure their continued availability for future generations of stamp collectors, researchers, and historians.

## 🌐 The Five Collections

### 🎨 1. [Art on Stamps](https://vicman-ch.github.io/philatelic-heritage/collections/art/index.html)
Fine art, masters, and world-renowned masterpieces as reflected on postage stamps from around the globe. Almost 1000 worldwide members of the Art on Stamps club.

### 🇷🇴 2. [Romania as Shown by Its Stamps](https://vicman-ch.github.io/philatelic-heritage/collections/romania/index.html)
An exploration of Romanian history, geography, diverse culture, and national identity through philately. Study of some difficult modern stamp issues.

### 🏛️ 3. [Philatelic Webmasters Organization (PWO)](https://vicman-ch.github.io/philatelic-heritage/collections/pwo/index.html)
Documentation of international philatelic collaboration, reference materials, an over 350 list of philatelic webmasters and combating illegal stamp issues.

### ☭ 4. [Communism on Stamps](https://vicman-ch.github.io/philatelic-heritage/collections/communism/index.html)
An analytical look at the political history, propaganda, and visual culture of communism through its postal issues.

### 🌍 5. [Spanish Africa and Its Stamps](https://vicman-ch.github.io/philatelic-heritage/collections/africa/index.html)
The rich postal history, natural landscapes, and distinct culture of the former Spanish territories in Africa. Philatelic humor from the rec.collecting.stamps.discuss newsgroup.

## 🏛️ About the Preservation
The original layout, artwork, and historical context of these platforms have been preserved in this archive. Visitors can access the live preserved versions via GitHub Pages directly through this repository.
